# Starship Commander Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 23, 2026

Starship Commander does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The game does not use user accounts, analytics, advertising networks, tracking technologies, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or a remote gameplay backend. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the player's device.

If a player contacts support voluntarily, the information included in that message is used only to respond to the request and is not sold or used for advertising.

This policy may be updated if the game's data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted at this location.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support link on the Starship Commander App Store listing.
